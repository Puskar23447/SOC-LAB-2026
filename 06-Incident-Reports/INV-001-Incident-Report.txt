SOC INCIDENT REPORT

Incident ID: INV-001
Severity: High
Status: Closed - Lab Investigation

Incident:
Insecure NFS configuration was identified on the
Metasploitable machine.

Affected Host:
192.168.56.103

Finding:
The NFS export configuration showed:

/ *

This means the root directory was exported to all hosts.

Evidence:
- nfs-export.png
- nfs-root-access.png
- evidence-index.txt

Impact:
An improperly restricted NFS export may allow unauthorized
access to files on the system.

Other Observations:
- Telnet was open on port 23.
- SMB ports 139 and 445 were open.
- FTP services were available.
- Multiple other services were exposed.

Recommended Actions:
- Restrict NFS access to trusted hosts.
- Do not export the root directory.
- Disable unnecessary services.
- Disable Telnet and use SSH.
- Monitor unusual access and authentication attempts.

Conclusion:
The investigation identified an insecure NFS configuration
and several unnecessary exposed services. The lab was
performed in an isolated VirtualBox environment.
